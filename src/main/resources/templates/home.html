Welcome to Student Management System

The Student Management System is a web-based application designed to manage student information efficiently. It helps the college maintain student records in a centralized database.

Students can be registered, updated and deleted through this application. The system provides an easy way to maintain student information.

Our Services

1. Student Registration

Register new students by entering their personal details.

2. Update Student Details

Modify existing student information using the student ID.

3. Delete Student Records

Remove student records using their unique student ID.

4. College Information

Explore college details and available academic branches.

Student Information Managed by Our System

  • Student ID
  • Student Name
  • Branch
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number
  • Address
  • Date of Birth

Why Choose Our System?

  • Easy management of student records.
